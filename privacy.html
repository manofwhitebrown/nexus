Privacy

Last updated October 2026.

What Nexus stores

Your tasks, notes, links, projects and activity history. All of it is saved in your browser's local storage on this device.

What leaves your device

Nothing you write is sent to us or to any server. Nexus has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The only outside request is loading the Familjen Grotesk font from Google Fonts, which Google may log as a normal font request.

Your control

Use Settings to export a backup file, restore one, or delete everything. Clearing your browser data also removes your Nexus data, so export a backup first.

Backups and sharing

Backup files contain everything you saved, unencrypted. Keep them somewhere safe.

Photos, voice notes and files

Attachments are stored in your browser's IndexedDB on this device only. They are not included in backup files.

Ask Nexus (optional)

If you choose to use Ask Nexus, you enter your own Anthropic API key, which is stored on this device. When you ask a question, the question and the matching items from your notes are sent to Anthropic to generate an answer. Nothing is sent unless you press Ask.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the GitHub repository that hosts this app.
